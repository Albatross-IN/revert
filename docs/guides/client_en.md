# Your project with Revert Architects: one page for clients

**What it is for.** To see the drawings we need your go-ahead on, and the few things we are waiting
for from you. Nothing else: no long lists, no technical drawings.

## Two ways in
- **The link.** We send you a link on WhatsApp. Tap it: your project opens in the browser.
  Nothing to install, no password.
- **The app.** Install **Revert Architects**, choose your language, type your mobile number, and
  type the 4-digit code that comes on WhatsApp.

## What you will see
- **Waiting for you**: a drawing to approve, or a choice to make (a stone, a colour, a fitting),
  each with the date we need it by.
- **Your drawings**: the drawings we have shared with you. Always the latest revision.

## To approve a drawing
1. Tap the drawing. Tap "Open full screen" to look closely.
2. Happy with it? Tap **Approve**. Your name, the date and the time are recorded. No signature needed.
3. Want something changed? Write what, and tap **Ask for a change**. We send a new revision.

## To close an item
When you have chosen or done what was asked, tap **Mark as done**.

## Good to know
- Your family can use the same link.
- You see only your own project.
- Help: call the Revert office.
