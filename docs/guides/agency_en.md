# Revert app: one page for agencies

**What it is for.** The current drawing of your work, your points from site visits, and your bills
with Revert Architects. In Gujarati, Hindi or English.

## Sign in (one time)
1. Install **Revert Architects** from the Play Store (or the App Store).
2. Choose your language.
3. Type your mobile number: the same number Revert has for you.
4. A 4-digit code comes on WhatsApp. Type it. You are in.

## Every day
- **Home** shows two numbers: your points and your new drawings.
- **Drawings**: tap a drawing to open it full screen. You always get the latest revision.
  Revert sees that you opened it.
- **A doubt?** Open the drawing, type your question, add a photo from site, tap Send.
- **Your points**: what Revert noted for you on a site visit, with the date.

## Bills
- You upload a bill only when Revert asks. The request shows on Home under "Revert asks".
- Open it, type the bill number and the amount, take a photo of the bill, tap Submit.
- You will see: Submitted → Accepted → Part paid → Paid.
- If it comes back, the reason is written there. Correct it and submit again.

## Good to know
- No network at site? Open the app anyway: what you saw last is there.
- New phone or lost phone: sign in again with your number. Tell Revert if a phone is lost.
- Help: call your coordinator at Revert.
