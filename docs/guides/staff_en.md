# Revert desk and app: one page for the team

**One place for a project:** who is working, what is blocked, which drawing is current, what was
said on site, which bill is where. The desk (in the browser) and the app (on the phone) show the
same thing.

## Start
- **Desk:** log in at the office address; you land on Today.
- **App:** install Revert Architects, tap "Revert staff? Log in here", use your office login.

## Today
The things that need *you*, across all sites: a drawing to approve or release, an agency to decide,
a client point to confirm, a bill to review, an agency to chase. Each has its button. "Start" walks
you through them one by one.

## A project
- **Picture:** status, blockers (what holds what, who must act), next actions per party.
- **Work:** tap a room, tap a trade, set Not started / Working / Waiting / Done. Waiting asks for
  the cause (a drawing, a point, material, the client).
- **Drawings:** the designer uploads the PDF and sends it for approval; the principal approves;
  it is released to the agencies and, if marked, to the client. They get it on WhatsApp and always
  see the latest revision. You see who opened it.
- **MOM:** at site, start the visit on the phone. For each point: who it is for, the room, the
  text, a photo, the date. Before you leave the room, send each party its own PDF. No network?
  Keep writing; it goes out when the network is back.
- **Client:** invite the client's contact, copy the link that opens the project without the app.
- **Bills:** ask the agency for a bill; it uploads a photo from its app; accept it or return it
  with the reason. Payments and amounts are the owners'.

## New project
"New" → six steps: the project and client, the team, the rooms (a 2 or 3 BHK preset fills them),
the agencies per trade, the drawing list, a last look. About five minutes.

## Rules the system keeps for you
- An agency never sees an old revision, another agency's points, or anyone's amounts.
- A client sees only what is marked for the client, and client points after the office confirms them.
- Nothing is chased by itself: a reminder goes when you tap Remind.
