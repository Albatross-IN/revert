## Privacy

*Last updated: [date]*

This page is about the Revert Architects app and the web page some clients open from a link. They
are run by Revert Architects, [address], Ahmedabad. Questions: [e-mail], [phone].

### Who uses it
Our team, the agencies working on our sites, and our clients. Nobody else can sign in: a mobile
number works only if we have put it on a project.

### What we keep, and why

| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Your name and mobile number | To know who you are on a project, to send you the sign-in code and messages about your work on WhatsApp |
| What you write and upload: questions on a drawing, photos from site, bills | It is the work itself. The people on that project who need it can see it |
| That you opened a drawing, and when; that a client approved one, with name, date and time | So that everyone knows which drawing is current and what was agreed |
| Your language and notification choices | To show the app in your language and to notify you only for what you chose |
| A name and an identifier of the phone you sign in on | To keep you signed in, and to let you sign a lost phone out |

We do not keep your contacts, your location, or anything else from your phone. The camera is used
only when you tap it. If you switch on the fingerprint or face lock, the check is done by your
phone; the app and our server never see it.

### Who sees what
- An agency sees its own points, the drawings released to it and its own bills. It never sees
  another agency's.
- A client sees the drawings marked for the client and the items waiting for the client.
- Amounts of bills and payments are seen by the owners of Revert Architects only.
- We do not sell your data or give it to advertisers. Messages reach you through WhatsApp, so
  WhatsApp handles them under its own terms.

### Where it is kept, and for how long
On a server we rent, reached over an encrypted connection. Project records are kept for as long as
we need them for the project and our legal duties. The copy the app keeps on your phone is removed
when you sign out, or from the app's settings ("Clear what is kept on this phone").

### Your choices
You can switch notifications off in the app. You can ask us what we hold about you, to correct it,
or to remove your sign-in and your personal details where the law lets us; write to [e-mail].

### Changes
If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
