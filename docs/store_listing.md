## Full description

Revert Architects designs homes, offices and clinics in Ahmedabad. This app is for the people who
build them with us: our team, the agencies on site, and our clients.

For agencies (carpenter, electrician, plumber, and every other trade)
• The latest drawing of your work, always the current revision. Old revisions are never shown.
• Your points from each site visit, with photo and date.
• Ask a question on a drawing, with a photo from site.
• Upload your bill when Revert asks for it, and see when it is accepted and paid.
• In Gujarati, Hindi or English.

For clients
• The drawings that need your approval. Approve with one tap, or say what should change.
• The few things we are waiting for from you, with dates.

For the Revert team
• Today: what needs you now, across all sites.
• Each project at a glance: what is blocked, who has to act.
• Site visits: write a point, add a photo, send each agency its own list before you leave the room.
  It works without network and sends when the network is back.

Sign-in is by your mobile number and a code sent on WhatsApp. Only people on a Revert project can
sign in.

## Data safety form (Google Play) and privacy labels (App Store)

| Is data encrypted in transit? | Yes (HTTPS) |
