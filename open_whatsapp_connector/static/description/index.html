WhatsApp Connector

Send and receive WhatsApp messages from your business system. Link a WhatsApp number by scanning a QR code; a small sidecar service on the server keeps the connection open.

Messaging

  • Text, images, documents, voice notes, polls, locations and contact cards
  • Long messages split cleanly, per-account sending limits against bans
  • Delivery and read receipts on every message

Inbox

  • WhatsApp conversations in the Discuss inbox, one per contact and account
  • Conversation assignment, labels and a customer timeline
  • Groups, communities and broadcast channels

Automation

  • Notification rules that message contacts when a record changes state
  • Campaigns, auto-replies and chatbots
  • Inbound rules that turn messages into leads, tickets or orders

Operations

  • Several WhatsApp numbers, each with its own settings and access rules
  • Dashboard, account diagnostics and a log of every message
