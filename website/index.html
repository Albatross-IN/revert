FAQ

Questions Clients Ask First

How much does an interior or renovation project cost?

It depends on the area, the scope and the materials you choose. After the site visit we share a room-wise bill of quantities, so you see the cost of every item before you commit.

How long will my project take?

It depends on the scope. With the design you get a written, stage-wise schedule, and photo updates after every site visit so you can follow it.

Can we keep living at home during the renovation?

Often, yes. Where possible we work room by room and tell you in advance which days a space cannot be used.

Can you work with our own carpenter or contractor?

Yes. We can share drawings and supervise work by your team, or handle every trade ourselves on a turnkey basis.

What happens after handover?

We walk through the finished space with you, close every snag on the list, and come back for a follow-up visit.
